Shipping Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Free Shipping

Orders totaling $250 or more qualify for free standard shipping within the continental United States. No promo code is needed — free shipping is applied automatically at checkout when your subtotal reaches the threshold.

2. Flat Rate Shipping

Orders under $250 ship for a flat rate of $9.99 via standard ground shipping. Delivery typically occurs within 3–7 business days after order processing.

3. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed the next business day. You will receive a tracking number via email once your order ships.

4. Discreet Packaging

All orders ship in plain, discreet packaging with no indication of the contents. Return labels display our business name only. We take your privacy seriously at every stage of fulfillment.

5. Contact Us

For shipping inquiries, reach us at info@puritylabs.org.
